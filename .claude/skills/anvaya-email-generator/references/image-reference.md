## Usage Tips

1. **Hero Images:** Use landscape-oriented images for hero sections
2. **Section Images:** Use images that match the specific offering in each section
3. **Consistency:** Try to maintain visual consistency across sections of the same email
4. **Quality:** All images are high-resolution and optimized for email use
5. **Alt Text:** Always include descriptive alt text for accessibility

## Image Dimensions

Most images are 1440×768 or similar landscape ratios, perfect for:
- Hero sections: Use at full width or resize to 640×400px
- Content sections: Use at 300×225px (half-width layout)
